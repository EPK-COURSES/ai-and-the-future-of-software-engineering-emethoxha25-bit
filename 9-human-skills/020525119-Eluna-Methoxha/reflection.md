### **Personal Reflection - Human Skills in the AI Era**



###### **What surprised me most?**

###### 

When I first started this research I honestly thought that AI was going to replace software engineers very soon and I was afraid because this is the subject that I am currently studying. I had heard many people talking about how AI can write code faster than humans and I was really worried about choosing software engineering as a career. However after doing this research my perspective changed completely. What surprised me most was discovering that human skills are actually becoming more valuable in the AI era, not less. I did not except to find that companies like Gartner are saying that leadership, ethical reasoning and critical thinking are now more important than ever. This was a big surprise for me.



###### **What concerns me about AI in Software Engineering?**



Even though I feel more positive about the future after this research, I still have some serious concerns. My biggest worry is that software engineers might become too dependent on AI tools without truly understanding the code they are producing. When an engineer just copies AI generated code without understanding it, mistakes can happen that are very difficult to fix later. Another concern I have is about ethics. AI systems can produce biased or incorrect results and if engineers do not review and question these results carefully, harmful software could be released to real users. I think this is a responsibility that engineers must take very seriously. Finally I am also concerned about junior developers and students like me. If AI handles all the basic coding tasks, how will begineers learn and grow their skills? This is something the industry needs to think carefully.



###### **What opportunities do I see?**

###### 

Despite my concerns I also see many exciting opportunities that AI brings to software engineering. One of the biggest opportunities that I see is that engineers who will learn how to work with AI will be able to build bigger and better software than ever. AI can handle repetitive and boring tasks with frees engineers to focus on more creativity and interesting parts of their work. I also see opportunities in new roles that are emerging because of AI such as AI ethics oversight, AI systems design and prompt engineering. These are completely new career paths that did not exist a few years ago. For a student like me who is entering the field now this is actually a very exciting time to be learning software engineering.



###### **Which skills do I think will remain important?**



After completing this research I personally believe that communication and critical thinking are the two most important skills for the future. No matter how advanced AI becomes someone still needs to sit with a client, understand their real needs and translate those needs into a technical solution. AI cannot do this because it cannot truly understand human emotions, business contexts and real world problems the way a human can. I also think that ethical reasoning will become extremely important. As AI systems become more powerful the decisions engineers make about how to use them will have bigger and bigger impacts on society. Engineers who can think ethically and responsibly will be the ones that companies trust the most. Finally I believe creativity and systems thinking will remain irreplaceable because AI can only work within existing patterns while humans can imagine entirely new solutions.



###### **How will I personally adapt?**



As a software engineering student at Epoka University I have been thinking a lot about how to prepare myself for this changing industry. My plan is to focus not only on learning programming languages and technical skills but also on developing strong communication and teamwork abilities. I want to practice working in teams, presenting ideas clearly and understanding business needs alongside technical requirements. I also plan to learn how to use AI tools effectively so that I can use them to increase my productivity rather than replace my thinking. Most importantly I want to always make sure that I truly understand every solution I produce whether it comes from AI or from my own coding. I believe this combination of human skills and AI knowledge will make me a valuable engineer in the future.



###### **Did my opinion change after research?**



Yes, my opinion change significantly after doing this research. Before I started I was genuinely worried that choosing software engineering as a career was a risky decision because of how fast AI is advancing. I thought maybe in 10 years there would be no need for human engineers at all. But after reading reports from Gartner, Built In, Tech Mahindra and other sources now I understand that the reality is much more nuanced than that. AI will change software engineering but it will not end it. The engineers who will struggle are those who refuse to adapt and who only see AI as a threat. The engineers who will thrive are those who embrace AI as a powerful tool while continuing to develop the human skills that no algorithm can replicate. I now feel much more confident and excited about my future in software engineering.
